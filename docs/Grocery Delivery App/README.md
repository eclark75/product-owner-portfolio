
# Grocery Delivery App

## Product Owner Portfolio Project

The Grocery Delivery App is a self-directed Product Owner portfolio project demonstrating how Agile product management practices can be applied to a consumer-focused digital product.

The project was developed after gaining experience in consumer product operations, customer service, workflow coordination, and Agile product ownership.

## Why I Chose This Product

My background includes hands-on experience in consumer product operations at Reggio's, where I worked with production, customers, retail-related needs, workflow coordination, and day-to-day operational challenges.

I used that experience as a foundation for thinking about a digital grocery ordering experience from both the customer and operational perspectives.

The goal of this project was to design a simple grocery ordering experience that allows customers to:

- Browse grocery products
- Search for products
- Add products to a cart
- Complete checkout
- Receive order confirmation

## Product Management Approach

I applied Agile Product Owner practices throughout the project, including:

- Product vision
- Customer/user personas
- Product requirements
- User stories
- Acceptance criteria
- Product backlog
- Backlog prioritization
- Sprint planning
- Product roadmap
- Wireframing
- Agile/Scrum practices

## My Role

**Product Owner**

I was responsible for defining the product direction, identifying user needs, organizing and prioritizing the backlog, creating user stories and acceptance criteria, planning sprint work, and translating the product vision into a proposed digital experience.

## Connection to My Professional Background

This project demonstrates how my previous operational experience can transfer into product management.

At Reggio's, I developed experience understanding customer needs, coordinating workflows, supporting consumer products, and working through operational challenges.

My experience at Primo Water/Hinckley Springs further developed my ability to identify workflow problems, troubleshoot issues, prioritize competing work, maintain documentation, and improve operational efficiency while meeting quality goals.

I applied those transferable skills to a digital product scenario through Agile product management practices.
