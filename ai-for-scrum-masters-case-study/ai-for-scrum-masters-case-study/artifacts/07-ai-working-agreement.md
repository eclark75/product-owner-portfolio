# 07 · Team AI Working Agreement (Regulated Environment)

The course covered **using AI ethically in daily Scrum practice**. In banking, that starts with clear rules the whole team agrees to before anyone uses AI.

## We use AI to
- Draft user stories, acceptance criteria and story slices for refinement
- Draft Sprint Goal options and identify risks during planning
- Spot patterns on the sprint board (team-level only)
- Group Retrospective notes into themes
- Draft stakeholder updates for the Product Owner to review

## We never use AI to
- Make prioritization, scope or release decisions
- Approve work or decide whether something is "Done"
- Evaluate, rank or monitor individual team members
- Write regulatory or legal language that skips Compliance review
- Send anything to stakeholders or customers without human review

## Data rules
1. **Approved tools only.** We use only AI tools approved by the bank's technology and risk policies.
2. **No customer data.** Never enter names, account or card numbers, transaction details or case records.
3. **No confidential information.** No internal system names, security details, credentials or non-public strategy.
4. **Anonymize first.** Retrospective notes and board summaries are anonymized before AI use.
5. **When in doubt, leave it out.** If we are unsure whether information is allowed, we don't paste it, and we ask.

## Quality rules
- Every AI output is a **draft** until a named person accepts it.
- Facts, numbers and policy statements from AI are verified or removed.
- Missing information becomes a question for the right owner, not a guess.
- We note in the story or document when AI helped draft it, so there is a clear trail.

## Review
We revisit this agreement every third Retrospective, or sooner when the bank's AI policy changes.

*Agreed by the Card Services Scrum team, Sprint 1.*
