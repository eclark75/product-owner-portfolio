# AI for Scrum Masters: Applied Case Study

**How a Scrum Master and Product Owner can use generative AI responsibly on a digital banking Scrum team.**

I completed the Scrum Alliance **AI for Scrum Masters** microcredential and built this case study to show how I apply it in practice. It follows a fictional mobile banking team through one product goal: launching self-service card disputes in a mobile app. Each step shows the prompt I used, the AI draft, and the decision the team made.

## Key takeaways

- **AI drafts, people decide.** Every AI output in this case study is reviewed, edited or rejected by the team.
- **Guardrails come first.** No customer data, approved tools only, human review before anything is shared.
- **AI makes mistakes.** It missed accessibility and added an unsupported compliance claim; review caught both.
- **Improve the prompt, not just the output.** A Retrospective led to a better prompt for every future story.
