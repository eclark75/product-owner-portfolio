## 🚀 Featured Case Studies

### 1. Hinckley Springs: Refurbishment Pipeline & Quality Architecture
* **Domain:** Industrial Operations, Quality Engineering & Workflow Optimization
* **Core Focus:** Continuous-flow manufacturing, WIP bottleneck elimination, closed QA feedback loops.
* **Key Achievements:**
  * Scaled steady-state velocity to **90 units/shift** with clear handoff criteria.
  * Architected a closed QA rework loop achieving an SLA of **zero field defects**.
  * Designed end-to-end assembly workflows and decision gates using Miro and Git-based documentation.
* **Artifacts:** Process flow diagram, QA loop architecture, triage criteria.

### 2. Direct Store Delivery (DSD) Mobile Inventory & Order Portal
* **Domain:** Supply Chain Logistics, Retail Distribution & Field Operations
* **Core Focus:** Offline-first mobile check-in engine, real-time discrepancy alerts, and electronic Proof of Delivery (ePOD).
* **Key Achievements:**
  * Authored complete **PRD** and Agile **User Stories Backlog** using **BDD/Gherkin** (`Given-When-Then`) acceptance criteria.
  * Designed sequence workflow for offline barcode caching (GS1-128) and automated 5-second background sync.
  * Targeted a **35% reduction** in dock intake check-in time and **50% decrease** in delivery dispute claims.
* **Artifacts:** 
  * [Product Requirements Document (PRD)](case-studies/01-dsd-inventory-portal/PRD.md)
