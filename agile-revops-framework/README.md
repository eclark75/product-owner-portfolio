# Agile Revenue Operations (RevOps) Framework & Case Study

An operational framework and blueprint applying Scrum product management principles to Revenue Operations (RevOps) and Go-To-Market (GTM) delivery pipelines.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Revenue Operations often suffers from being treated as a reactive "help desk" for ad-hoc sales, marketing, and customer success requests. This case study demonstrates an operational model designed to treat revenue systems, workflows, and commercial tooling as an **internal product** using core Scrum disciplines.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives
* **Eliminate Pipeline Bottlenecks:** Establish clear handoffs and service level agreements (SLAs) across marketing, sales, and fulfillment.
* **Objective Prioritization:** Implement an objective scoring system to prevent the "loudest voice in the room" from dictating operational priorities.
* **Predictable Delivery Cadence:** Protect team bandwidth with structured sprint cycles while maintaining capacity for real-time operational escalations.

---

## ⚙️ Core Components

### 1. ICE-R Prioritization Matrix
Incoming operational requests and tech stack updates are evaluated across four weighted dimensions before entering sprint planning:
* **Impact:** How significantly does this remove a blocker or improve operational efficiency?
* **Confidence:** How certain are we of the requirements, technical scope, and expected outcome?
* **Ease:** What level of operational and technical effort is required?
* **Revenue Risk:** What is the financial or SLA exposure if this ticket is delayed?

### 2. Capacity Model (70 / 30 Split)
To prevent mid-sprint derailment from urgent operational issues:
* **70% Committed Bandwidth:** Dedicated to planned strategic workflow improvements, backlog items, and system upgrades.
* **30% Ring-Fenced Buffer:** Reserved for critical day-to-day escalations, urgent sales blockers, and immediate triage.

### 3. Cross-Functional Governance & SLAs
* **Sprint Cadence:** 2-week iteration cycles with structured sprint planning, backlog grooming, and sprint reviews.
* **Standardized Handoff Checklists:** Documented definition of ready (DoR) and definition of done (DoD) to eliminate dropped handoffs between departments.
* **User Stories & Acceptance Criteria:** Business requests converted into clear functional requirements to reduce rework.
